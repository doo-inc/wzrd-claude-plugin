# WZRD brand system

Use this reference for WZRD visual design, motion, interaction, naming, and copy. It condenses the maintained WZRD Brand Guidelines v2.0.

## Core idea

WZRD is the intelligence layer for interactive experiences. A person describes what they need; WZRD configures an experience people can talk to. Documents, forms, sheets, and slides are possible surfaces, not the product category. The presence should feel calm, capable, attentive, ambient, and quietly magical.

Mission: replace static interfaces with one configurable assistant that talks, listens, and adapts. The user remains the builder; WZRD is already on their side.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Soft authority. Direct, warm, and curious without sounding eager.
- WZRD speaks as **we**, reflecting the user and WZRD building together. Avoid “How can I help?” and generic assistant introductions.
- Ask plainly: “What do you want to make?” Explain plainly: “It’s ready.”
- Prefer: build, configure, surface, ambient, intelligence, presence, choose, listen, the orb, today, “here’s what we can do.”
- Avoid brand language such as: leverage, empower, unlock, revolutionize, AI-powered, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, helper, bot, copilot, or agent.
- If a 10-year-old cannot understand the UI copy in one read, simplify it. Use normal navigation names such as Home, Your work, Build, Preview, Share, and Responses.
- The magic comes from confidence and behavior, not wizard jokes, wand emoji, hype, or self-description.

## Logo

- Use the single canonical WZRD orb: three translucent lavender petals around a luminous core. Do not invent lockups or redraw it.
- Keep the orb upright and circular. Product motion may animate it; static uses stay at rest.
- Minimum size: 24 px digital or 12 mm print. Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the orb diameter on every side.
- Never recolor outside the lavender-purple glass family, stretch, flip, add a ring, or add external glow, halo, overlay, or drop shadow.
- On a saturated or busy field, place it on a calm `#F5F5F5` tile or disc rather than drawing a frame.

## Color and gradients

Core colors:

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Lavender | `#AC73E6` |
| Deep purple | `#2D004D` |
| Ink | `#171717` |
| Light background | `#F5F5F5` |
| Elevated surface | `#FBFBFB` |
| Subtle surface | `#ECECEC` |

- Default to soft neutral light surfaces; use ink for dark mode.
- Gradients are soft, diffused, vertical, and eased through intermediate tones. Edges dissolve into the surrounding surface.
- Never apply gradients to type. Avoid diagonal ramps, hard stops, sharp geometric decoration, or fully saturated slabs.
- Decorative color should feel like blurred weather and should not sit behind body text.

## Typography and icons

- Use Plus Jakarta Sans for display, UI, and body: weights 400, 500, 600, 700, and 800.
- Use negative tracking for display sizes, positive tracking for small eyebrow labels, and normal tracking elsewhere.
- Icons are line-only with rounded caps and joins. Use deep purple on light surfaces and lavender on dark surfaces. Standard display sizes are 16, 24, and 32 px.

## Components and interaction

- Core primitives are a floating capsule nav island, glass choice chips, glassmorphic cards, and rounded gradient buttons.
- Controls are pills or circles, never squared. Every interactive element must visibly respond on hover.
- Primary buttons are deep purple with light text at rest; the soft Spell gradient arrives on hover with a 2 px lift. Do not leave gradients permanently active at rest.
- Keep card effects restrained: translucent lavender, inset highlight, soft border, and no hard glow.

## Motion

WZRD breathes. Motion is slow, organic, and non-linear. Prefer quart-out or exponential easing. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

- Breathe: slow morphing with meaningful rest.
- Drift: varied ambient motion rather than synchronized mechanical loops.
- Reach: blurred, softly staggered entrances; faster reverse exits.
- Pulse: irregular voice cadence rather than a perfect sine wave.
- Magnetize: subtle cursor response that returns gently home.
- Caption reveal: align word-level reveal to actual audio timing.

Judge motion by whether it feels attentive and calm, not by how many effects it contains.
